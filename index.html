Questions, answered

Ads, price, platforms and offline play

What is ASApps Games?

ASApps Games is an independent mobile game studio. It publishes five free puzzle and card games for iPhone and iPad — Sudoku No Pop-up Ads, Color Blocks, Block Blast Puzzle Jewel, Sudoku Blocks Tetra, and Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider — all of which run without pop-up ads, full-screen interruptions, or required in-app purchases. Sudoku No Pop-up Ads, Color Blocks, and Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider are also available on Android via Google Play.

Do ASApps Games show pop-up ads?

No. There are no pop-up ads and no forced ad breaks during gameplay in any ASApps Games title. The games are free and supported by a small unobtrusive banner, plus optional short ads you can choose to watch — and every core feature is available without any required purchase.

Are the games free to download?

Yes. All five games are free to download and play on the App Store, and the three Android titles are free on Google Play. There are no required in-app purchases.

Are ASApps Games available on Android?

Three games are available on Android via Google Play: Sudoku No Pop-up Ads, Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider, and Color Blocks. The other two — Block Blast Puzzle Jewel and Sudoku Blocks, Tetra — are currently iOS only (iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch).

Do the games work offline?

Yes. All ASApps Games titles are fully playable offline — no Wi-Fi or mobile data is required.

Who develops ASApps Games?

ASApps Games is an independent mobile game studio. Its games are published under a single App Store developer account, with Sudoku, Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider, and Color Blocks also on Google Play for Android.
